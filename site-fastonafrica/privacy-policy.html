Who we are

Fast on Africa (FOA) is a service of Media Icons Limited, Lagos, Nigeria, a Media Icons Africa company. We are the data controller for personal data collected through fastonafrica.com and our onboarding, billing and payout processes. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your rights under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and, where they apply to you, the data protection laws of other countries.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only as needed with:

  • payment partners such as Paystack and Flutterwave, to take payments and make payouts;
  • hosting, video and streaming providers that run our website and Channels;
  • advertising and sales partners, who receive aggregated Channel statistics, not your personal details;
  • other Media Icons Africa companies, such as Asiko and Media Icons Digital, to deliver the services;
  • professional advisers, auditors and authorities where the law requires.

Each provider may only use your data to provide its service to us.

International transfers

Some of our providers and sales partners are outside Nigeria, for example in the United Kingdom, United States, Canada and the European Union. When your data is transferred abroad, we use providers with appropriate safeguards, such as contractual protections, as the Nigeria Data Protection Act requires.

How long we keep it

  • Onboarding applications that don't go ahead: up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Customer, billing and payout records: for the life of the contract and up to 6 years after it ends, for tax and legal purposes.
  • Marketing preferences: until you opt out.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your personal data;
  • correct anything inaccurate or incomplete;
  • delete your data where we no longer need it;
  • restrict or object to certain uses, including marketing;
  • send your data to you or another organisation in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent where we rely on it.

Send requests to the contact below. We reply within 30 days. If you're unhappy with our response, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Security

We use access controls, two-factor login on our systems, encrypted connections (HTTPS), and limited staff access to protect your data. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we'll tell you and the regulator as the law requires.

Cookies and storage

fastonafrica.com uses only what's needed to run the site and remember basic preferences in your browser. We don't use advertising cookies on this site. If that changes, we'll update this policy and ask for consent where required.

Children

Our services are for businesses and adults. We don't knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows the latest version, and we'll notify customers of significant changes by email.
