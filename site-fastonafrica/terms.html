The service

FOA turns your content library into a scheduled, always-on streaming ("FAST") or satellite channel. Depending on your plan, we encode your content, build a 24/7 schedule and programme guide, insert ad breaks, run playout, and distribute your channel to Asiko.africa and the other destinations your plan includes. Plan features are as described on fastonafrica.com at the time you sign up.

Free trial

New FAST channels get a 30-day free trial: one channel on Asiko.africa, up to 100 hours of content, with viewing and ad reports. No card is needed. At the end of the trial you choose a plan, or we pause the channel. Your content is not deleted when a channel is paused unless you ask us to delete it.
