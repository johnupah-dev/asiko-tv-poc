Completing this page

LinkedIn blocks automated access, so profile data can't be pulled in directly — and bios for named people won't be invented. To finish this page, for each person send:

  • Full name and preferred title
  • Their LinkedIn "About" text (or a 2–3 sentence bio) — it will be edited to a consistent house style
  • LinkedIn URL and any relevant past roles / companies
  • Headshot (optional) — can be added later

Paste them in any format; they'll be slotted into the right division and matched in tone.

Why this matters

  • For investors: the risk question "who builds and runs this?" is answered — named leads in technology, content and commercial, with legal and finance forming. Not a one-person bet.
  • For content owners: a real content team with industry standing, not a tech company guessing at programming.
  • For advertisers: a commercial team led by a 30-year media-sales operator — the category-exclusive sponsorship model is sold by people who have sold at this level before.
  • For the build: four in-house technology people means the MVP and beyond are executed by the team, not dependent on any single outside party.
