What could still gate everything

The schedule assumes DNS lands and one of {Eyevinn billing / KVM8 SSH} arrives by Sun–Mon. Every day those slip, the rest of the week slips with them.

Content rights and the Enveu decision are the two things that can gate everything past week 1 — neither is a coding problem, and both are yours to move.
