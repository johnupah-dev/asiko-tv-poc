Channel Owner Guide

Content delivery spec

Exactly what to send us so your channel goes on air first time: files, ad breaks, metadata, logo and ident.

1 · Video & audio files

Send your best master. We re-encode everything to one Asiko profile. What matters is that your files are clean, consistent and follow the rules below.

ItemRequiredNotes
ContainerMP4 or MOVOne file per programme or episode. No split parts.
Video codecH.264 (or ProRes / H.265 mezzanine)High bitrate is fine. Minimum 8 Mbps at 1080p.
Resolution1920×1080 (preferred) or 1280×72016:9. No letterbox burned into a 4:3 frame. Don't upscale SD to HD.
Frame rate25 fps, constantOr your native rate (24 / 29.97 / 30), but the same across all your files. No variable frame rate (common with phone footage).
ScanProgressiveDe-interlace before sending.
AudioAAC or PCM, stereo, 48 kHzOne stereo mix. Dialogue and music on the same track. No silent or mono-in-one-ear tracks.
Loudness−23 LUFS integrated (±1), true peak ≤ −1 dBTPStops your channel being louder or quieter than the one next to it.
Clean start / end1–2 s of black at head and tailNo colour bars, countdown clocks or slates inside the programme file.
On-screen graphicsNo channel logo burned inWe add your logo consistently at encode (see §4). Subtitles burned in are fine if the programme needs them.
Ad breaksNo ads inside the fileLeave the programme clean. Tell us the break points instead (§2).

2 · Ad-break cue points

Mark where a break can go: natural pauses, scene changes, act ends. We insert ads there, not in the middle of a line of dialogue.

  • Roughly one break every 12–15 minutes of programme.
  • Give each cue as a timecode (HH:MM:SS) from the start of the file, in the metadata sheet (§3).
  • If you already have fade-to-black break points, just list them. No re-edit needed.
  • Short programmes under 10 minutes need no cue. We place breaks between programmes.

4 · Logo, ident & slate

These make it look like your channel. Send them once, at the start.

  1. Channel logo, on-screen versionPNG with a transparent background, at least 400 px wide, readable on dark and light footage (a white or single-colour version works best). We place it in the top-right corner of every programme at encode time, the same on every screen. Also send an SVG if you have one, for the app and website.
  2. Channel identA 5–10 second video (same spec as §1) with your logo and channel name. It plays between programmes. Optional: a second ident for going into and coming out of breaks ("back after this…").
  3. Off-air slateA 1920×1080 still (or a 10 s video) with your logo. It is shown if a programme fails to load, instead of a black screen.
  4. Brand colourOne hex colour (e.g. #D8A94B). It is used for your channel's accents in the Asiko app.

5 · Schedule

Tell us the running order. The simplest form: a list of file names in the order they should play, which loops when it reaches the end. Put new episodes at the point in the list where you want them.

Aim for at least 20–40 hours of programming to start, so viewers don't see repeats the same day. Dated schedules (“Friday 20:00 premiere”) are coming. Tell us if you need them.

6 · How to deliver

  1. We send you an upload linkA private upload page for your channel's library. Or send a Google Drive / Dropbox / WeTransfer link and we upload for you.
  2. Upload brand assets firstThe logo has to be in place before your programmes are encoded. Otherwise they need re-encoding.
  3. Upload programmes + the metadata sheetFile names must match the sheet exactly.
  4. We check and confirmWithin 2 working days we tell you which files passed, and what to fix on any that didn't. Then your channel goes on air.

Top reasons files get sent back: variable frame rate (phone or screen recordings), a logo already burned in, audio on one side only, very quiet or clipped audio, colour bars or ads left in the file, and file names that don't match the sheet.
