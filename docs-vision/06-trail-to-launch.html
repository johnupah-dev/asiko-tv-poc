The trail from POC to launch

7 → 10 → 20 channels as a proof of concept, into an MVP the sales houses can plug into, a commercial pilot that proves the economics, and a full launch on a production build — with the money mapped at every step, and a plan for growing past FAST.

01

Where we are

The POC scales channels for free. Going from 7 to 10 to 20 is a config change, not a build — it's already done.

BuildWhat it isEffortWhat it proves

10 channelsSame, +3 slots (News, Sport, Kids)doneRoom for the first ten partner companies

What the POC is not: there is no real playout, no ad server, no revenue. Channels are HLS URLs played in a browser; the ad breaks and the earnings panel are a model. It is a demo — the right tool for meetings, not a platform.

One-time / capital

  • Content — licensing or production. The largest and most variable number, entirely your call. Public-domain + partner feeds keep it near zero to start; a real diaspora catalogue is five to six figures.
  • Enveu production build — $13,000 one-time (revised scope, Eyevinn-native, no AWS), committed at M8.
  • CTV apps — a web-based Roku channel can be ~$0–5k; native apps across Roku / Fire TV / Samsung / LG are $30k–120k if outsourced, or folded into the Enveu build.
  • Company, legal, ad-tech contracts — entity, privacy/consent, listing agreements, SSP onboarding. Low four figures.

The FAST revenue formula

Gross ad revenue ≈ viewer-hours × ad impressions per hour × (CPM ÷ 1000) × fill rate

Worked example (year-1, diaspora CTV assumptions): 24 impressions/viewer-hour × $10 CPM × 50% fill = $0.12 gross per viewer-hour.
Less SSP ~10% and a blended content-owner split (0% on owned channels, ~50% on partner channels) ≈ $0.06–0.08 net to Asiko per viewer-hour.
Less CDN at ~1.3 GB/viewer-hour × ~$0.01/GB ≈ $0.013 → contribution ≈ $0.05–0.07 per viewer-hour before fixed costs.

Break-even is a viewer-hours number, not a channel-count number. Roughly 60k–150k viewer-hours a month covers Phase-2 fixed costs — about 3,000–10,000 regular viewers. CPM, fill and watch-time each swing the result ±50%. Adding channels raises the ceiling; it does not move break-even. Audience acquisition and content are the real spend — plan for marketing, not just infrastructure.

Growing past FAST — hybrid monetisation

FAST stays the base and the funnel. Each new model is a layer on top — most reuse the same video and ad plumbing; the harder ones add payments, accounts and DRM.

LayerAdds (revenue)Needs (new tech)LiftMain risk
FAST linearAd revenue, rev-share with channel partners— (this is the platform)—Fill rate & CPM in a niche market
AVOD on-demandHigher CPMs (better targeting), more watch-time per userVOD catalogue + CMS, mid-roll cue points on assetsLow — reuses the ad stackContent rights for on-demand windows
TVOD / EST
“Asiko Cinemas”		Transaction revenue per rental / purchase; premium-title dealsPayments, a storefront, entitlement service, studio-grade DRM (Widevine / FairPlay / PlayReady)Medium–HighDRM & studio terms; conversion from a free audience
SVOD tierRecurring subscription revenue; predictable ARPUSubscription billing, accounts / auth, churn & dunning ops, more premium content spendHighChurn; needs enough exclusive content to be worth paying for
Hybrid (all)Diversified — ads + transactions + subscriptionsAll the above, integrated behind one account & one player—Operational complexity; each line needs its own discipline

The build sequence is deliberate: AVOD on-demand is nearly free to add and lifts ad revenue, so it comes first. TVOD / Asiko Cinemas is the first real new capability (payments + DRM) and a natural premium play for African cinema. SVOD comes last — it is a different business discipline and only works once you have an audience and exclusive content worth a monthly fee.

06

What this means for you now

  • Phase 0 is basically done — finish asiko.africa (DNS) and you have your meeting demo, 20 channels, for near zero.
  • Phase 1 needs two unlocks from you — playout access (Eyevinn billing or KVM8 SSH) and first content/partner feeds. Cost is hundreds a month, not thousands.
  • The Enveu $13k and the CTV-app spend wait until Phase 2 — committed against real pilot numbers, not a forecast.
  • Budget for audience, not servers. Break-even is ~60k–150k viewer-hours/month; getting there is a marketing and content spend, and it is the real number to plan around.
  • Monetisation growth is upside, not the plan. Get FAST working and funded first; add AVOD-on-demand, then Asiko Cinemas, then SVOD, each only when the layer below is earning.
