For investors

Have / ready

  • Working branded demo — 22 channels + 2 live, Asiko-skinned, organised Browse view with genre rows, competitions and voting.
  • Monetisation model, visualised — live revenue panel: fill, eCPM, gross, Direct / Programmatic / House split.
  • The document set — revised architecture, trail-to-launch with financials, onboarding & ad-revenue, design spec, week-1 plan. All in the repo and as shareable pages.
  • Phased plan with numbers — POC → MVP → pilot → launch, cost ladder, break-even in viewer-hours, monetisation growth path.

Must fix before the meeting

  • The demo on the brand domain — see blockers above.
  • The ask You — how much you're raising, use of funds, valuation. I can structure it once you set the numbers.
  • Team slide You — the Media Icons team is named (commercial, 4 in technology, content; legal & finance forming) — see the Team doc. Left to do: collect each person's LinkedIn bio and add headshots.
  • Content strategy, one page You — the biggest risk. Credible mix: partner feeds + a licensed diaspora library + owned production.
  • "Why Asiko wins" You — one slide vs GenXT, Kulture Streams, Edo Kulture Streams and the global FAST players. Diaspora focus, the culture, first-mover in the niche.
  • Traction proof, however small — one real channel running, or letters of intent from the 10 companies. Turns a pitch into evidence.

For prospective channel owners

Have / ready

  • "List Your Channel" pack — feed spec, SCTE-35, ad policy, EPG, rights, brand assets, the 5-step onboarding flow.
  • Their slot, visible — the Browse view shows genre rows with "List your channel" tiles; competitions have a dedicated row.
  • Revenue-share model explained — clean feed / reserved pods / baked-in, with typical splits.

Must fix before signing anyone

  • A listing-agreement template You + lawyer — a contract, not just the pack.
  • A real test-channel environment Me — take their HLS feed and show it running end to end. Needs playout access (blockers above).
  • Rate card & rev-share terms in writing You — specifics, not ranges.
  • A monthly-statement mock Me — what reporting they receive.

Legal & ops — before real ads run

  • Privacy Policy Lawyer — mandatory. Ad targeting = personal data under UK/EU GDPR from day one.
  • Cookie / consent banner (CMP) Me + policy — mandatory in UK/EU before programmatic ads. The POC has none.
  • Terms of Use Lawyer — liability, acceptable use, content disclaimers.
  • Company entity + bank + billing You — needed to contract with an SSP / ad server.
  • B2B pipeline tracker (light CRM) You — for the 10 companies, advertisers, competition entrants. A shared sheet is enough to start.

Legal pages live canonically on asiko.live but must be linked from inside the app — that's where data is collected. Ad rate cards and business terms stay as sales collateral, not consumer legal.

Roadmap — acknowledge, don't hide

These are expected gaps at POC stage. The trail-to-launch doc frames them as deliberate phases — say so in the room rather than gloss over them.

  • CTV apps — Roku, Fire TV, Samsung, LG. Where FAST money is. Phase 2.
  • Real server-side ad insertion (SSAI) — Eyevinn SGAI + an ad decision server. Phase 1–2.
  • CMS for scheduling & channel ops — Enveu scope, or built. Phase 2.
  • Live: DVR / catch-up / SCTE-35 ad breaks — not off-the-shelf; needs building. Phase 2–3.
  • Real voting backend — the competition rows are a mock today; a real service drops into that seam. MVP+.
  • AVOD on-demand → TVOD "Asiko Cinemas" → SVOD tier — the monetisation growth path. Upside, not the plan.

What only you can decide

  • The raise — amount, valuation, use of funds.
  • Enveu — yes or no — the $13k build partner, committed against Phase-2 data.
  • Content — what actually plays: deals, licences, owned production.
  • Team — hires, or lean on a build partner + managed ops.
  • Territory & entity — where Asiko is registered; which markets first.
