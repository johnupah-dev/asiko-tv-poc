List your channel on Asiko

Asiko TV is a free, ad-supported streaming home for African audiences across the UK, US, Canada and Europe. Here is exactly what we need from you to launch, and how the ad revenue is shared.

01

What listing gets you

  • Distribution to the whole Asiko audience — web now, connected-TV apps (Roku, Fire TV, Samsung, LG) as they roll out.
  • A share of the advertising revenue your channel generates — see §3.
  • No cost to list. You keep your content, your brand and your rights. Asiko integrates your feed; it does not take ownership of anything.
  • A guide slot, channel number and on-screen branding in the Asiko app.
02

The one thing that most affects your revenue: send a clean feed with SCTE-35, or reserved pods. A fully baked-in feed can't be measured or optimised on our side, so it earns the least (see §3).

CDN delivery cost (serving your channel to viewers) is carried by Asiko and factored into the split. Direct sponsorship that Asiko sells around your channel — a presenting sponsor, guide placement — is additional and not shared.

04

What happens after you submit

  1. Review & agreement — we confirm the feed spec, ad policy and rights, and sign a short listing agreement.
  2. Normalise — we pull your HLS, condition it to one profile, and validate or add the SCTE-35 markers. Your feed is not re-encoded end to end unless the profile is inconsistent.
  3. Test channel — your channel runs on an Asiko test environment, end to end, with a test ad break. You get a link to review it.
  4. Your sign-off — you confirm playback, branding, guide data and ad placement look right.
  5. Go live — the channel gets its number and guide slot on Asiko. Reporting starts from day one; first statement at the end of the month.

Typical time from a complete submission to a live channel is days, not weeks, when the feed is clean and the paperwork is quick.
