POC → MVP

From the 22-channel POC to a real MVP

The POC proves the idea in a browser. The MVP is the smallest version that is real — real channels, real ads, real reporting, that a sales house can plug into and a partner can go live on.

1 · What actually changes

LayerPOC todayMVP
ChannelsHLS URLs played in the browser; "linear" faked by seeking a loop to the wall clockReal 24/7 linear origin — Channel Engine (VOD2Live), 5–10 channels, from real content
AdsSimulated break every 150s; the revenue panel is a modelServer-side ad insertion (Eyevinn SGAI) firing on real SCTE-35, calling an ad decision server — first a test tag, then a real SSP
EPGComputed from a flat program listGenerated from a real dated schedule, delivered to the app
AnalyticsSample data in the Analytics previewReal player beacons + ad-server + CDN logs → a monthly statement per channel
StateAll in the browser's localStorageA small backend for schedules, config, vote counts, analytics ingest
Front endDesktop-first single pageHardened: real mobile layout, error handling, consent banner, Terms/Privacy links, no simulated panels in the public build
Domaingithub.ioasiko.africa with HTTPS, plus one CTV surface (web-based Roku or a PWA)
OpsNoneMonitoring + alerting, as-run logging, a defined on-call answer

What carries over unchanged: the player, the Browse view, the brand, the channels.json shape, the whole design. The MVP swaps the fake layers for real ones behind the same interface.

4 · Timeline & effort

Roughly 6–8 weeks from the two unlocks landing, most of it my build time, with your input on content, programming and the legal pack.

  • Weeks 1–2: W1 one real channel end to end · W7 asiko.africa live · W5 mobile layout + consent
  • Weeks 3–4: W1 clone to 5–10 channels · W2 real content in · W4 real schedules/EPG · W3 test ad tag proving a break
  • Weeks 5–6: W3 real SSP wired · W6 beacons + first statement · W8 light CMS · one CTV surface
  • Weeks 7–8: W9 legal linked · W10 monitoring · hardening · a partner goes live on a real channel

Cost stays modest — hundreds of dollars a month (playout, CDN, storage), plus one-off content/transcode and any legal fees. The big cheques (Enveu build, CTV apps, real content spend) are the pilot phase, committed against MVP numbers.

5 · In and out of MVP

In

  • 5–10 real linear channels, 24/7, from real content, on asiko.africa
  • Real SSAI with real demand; SCTE-35 from real schedules
  • Real EPG; hardened web app with a proper mobile layout; consent + legal links
  • Real measurement → a monthly per-channel statement
  • One CTV surface; a light CMS; monitoring
  • First invoice out; at least one partner channel genuinely live

Explicitly out — these are pilot / full-launch

  • Native CTV apps across Roku / Fire TV / Samsung / LG
  • A full multi-user CMS (Enveu scope)
  • Live DVR / catch-up / start-over
  • A real voting backend (the competition rows stay a mock)
  • The partner self-service portal (statements are PDF in MVP)
  • VOD / TVOD / SVOD
  • Scaling past ~10 channels

6 · Exit criteria — when MVP is done

  • 5–10 channels running 24/7 without manual intervention, with slate failover proven
  • An ad break rendered from a real SSP on a real channel, measured
  • A monthly statement a sales house looked at and trusted
  • The demo on asiko.africa, working on a phone
  • One external channel partner live end to end via their own feed
  • A first invoice issued — however small

Hit those and the pilot phase — CTV apps, real ad-sales pipeline, the Enveu build — is fundable against evidence instead of a forecast.
