01

Same brand, different job

asiko.live sells. asiko.africa is watched. They must be unmistakably the same company and structurally nothing alike.

Carries across (identical)Diverges (by design)
Logo, wordmark, "The Soul of Africa" lockupLayout — the app is chrome-light, the funnel is a scrolling marketing page
Palette — near-black ground, gold primary, warm white textDensity — the app is a 10-foot / lean-back surface, not a reading page
Type — Poppins display, Inter bodyInteraction — channel surfing, a guide, an ad-break state; no CTAs to "sign up"

02

Identity — locked to asiko.live

Colour

Lifted verbatim from the asiko.live stylesheet. The app uses the same token names.

TokenValueRole in the app
--bg#0a0a0cPlayer letterbox, base ground
--bg-soft#0f0f12Guide backdrop, sheets
--panel#17171bChannel rail cards, guide cells
--panel-2#1e1e23Active card, hover, pressed
--gold#d8a94bPrimary accent — active channel, focus ring, key numbers, LIVE-adjacent marks
--gold-bright#f3cd7eHover state of gold, headings within body
--gold-deep#a8792cPressed gold, section numerals
--white#f7f5f0All reading text and titles
--muted#a7a4acSecondary text — NEXT, timestamps, genres
--muted-2#716f78Tertiary — labels, disabled
--red#df3b34Reserved: the LIVE dot and the guide "now" line only

Gold is a UI and large-text colour, never body copy. Reading text is always --white on --bg. Red is reserved for "live now" signalling — nothing else.

Typography

  • Display — Poppins. Weights 800 for screen titles and sponsor names, 700 for section heads, 600 for channel names and buttons. Tight tracking (-0.02em) at large sizes, as on asiko.live.
  • Body / UI — Inter. 400–600. Programme titles, guide text, metadata.
  • Data — monospace. The on-screen clock, countdowns and timecodes use a tabular mono so digits don't jitter.
  • Both Poppins and Inter are open-licence (OFL) — no licensing cost, safe to embed in web and TV builds.

Logo & shape

  • Full lockup — gold angular "A" mark + ASIKO wordmark + THE SOUL OF AFRICA under-line, on dark only.
  • The "A" mark alone is the app icon, the favicon, and the small-space / TV-tile mark.
  • Clear space around the lockup = the height of the "A". Minimum wordmark height 16px web, 28px TV.
  • Radii 6 / 10 / 18px; buttons and badges are full pills (999px), matching asiko.live.

Voice

Warm, pan-African, plain. "One Africa. One Market. One Destination." · "The Soul of Africa." · "Built for how Africa actually watches." On-screen text is short and declarative — channel names, programme titles, "UP NEXT", "AD BREAK", never marketing lines inside the player.

Watch (default)

  • Full-bleed player on --bg. No persistent controls — they fade in on interaction and auto-hide after 3s.
  • Top bar: logo mark left · LIVE dot + network clock centre · Guide + audio toggle right.
  • Channel bug top-right: channel number in gold, name in white, a 2px gold left rule. Persistent, low opacity.
  • Lower-third: NOW tag (gold), programme title (Poppins 600), a thin progress line, then NEXT in muted with its start time.
  • Channel rail along the bottom: one card per channel — number, name, genre, now-playing line. Active card lifts to --panel-2 with a gold top border. Horizontal scroll / D-pad.
  • Optional sponsor strip (see §4) sits under the rail, not over the video.

Channel guide

  • Slides up over the player (player keeps running, ducked). --bg-soft at 96%.
  • Channels down the left (--panel label cells, gold left rule); programmes across in a time grid.
  • The live cell is --gold-wash with an inset gold rule. A single --red vertical "now" line crosses all rows.
  • Select a cell → tune to that channel and dismiss. Escape / Back closes.

Ad break

  • Full-screen branded slate — not a black gap. Gradient from --gold-deep-tinted black to --bg.
  • Top line (mono, muted): AD · n OF N · VIA {DIRECT SOLD | PROGRAMMATIC | HOUSE} — names the demand path, which is the monetisation story.
  • Sponsor name huge in gold (Poppins 800), one line of copy in white, a deal reference in muted.
  • Countdown ring top-right in gold. On completion, "Tuning" ident, then rejoin live.
  • This screen is the seam where real SSAI creative renders later — same layout, real ad.

Tuning & edge states

  • Channel change: ~600ms — black, logo mark, "TUNING · {NN NAME}".
  • First load: logo mark centred, "Bringing Africa online…", then straight into the last-watched channel.
  • Signal lost / offline: muted card, "Reconnecting…", auto-retry. Never a browser error.

Device matrix

Asiko is "every screen." The layout re-flows; the identity does not.

SurfaceLayoutInput & notes
Desktop webPlayer fills the viewport; rail along the bottom; guide slides upMouse + keys 1–9, G guide, M mute, ← → channel, F fullscreen. This is the POC today.
TabletSame as desktop; rail becomes a 2-row scroll; guide is full-screenTouch; tap video to reveal controls
Mobile (portrait)Player top (16:9), then NOW/NEXT, then a vertical channel list; guide is a full-screen sheetTouch; swipe up = guide, swipe L/R on player = change channel
TV / 10-footPlayer full-frame; rail overlays on Down; guide on Up/Menu. Type +2 steps up; 5% safe-area insetD-pad only. Focus is always a visible gold ring or a gold-bordered card — never rely on colour-fill alone. Roku / Fire TV / Android TV.
06

Motion & accessibility

Motion — functional only

  • Controls / lower-third: 150ms fade + 4px rise.
  • Guide: 220ms slide-up.
  • Ad countdown ring: linear, real-time.
  • Now-line: continuous, no easing.
  • No decorative motion, no parallax, no auto-playing hero flourishes — that lives on asiko.live, not in the player. Honour prefers-reduced-motion: cut all of the above to instant.

Accessibility

  • Body text is --white on --bg — ~17:1, well past AA. Gold on black clears AA for large text and UI.
  • Every focusable element shows a 2px gold ring, 2px offset. TV focus additionally moves a gold-bordered card — state is never colour-only.
  • Captions / subtitles toggle in the player; honour the platform caption style on TV.
  • Support an audio-description track where the source carries one.
  • Full keyboard and D-pad parity; the guide is a proper focus trap while open.
  • Live region announces channel changes and "Ad break — 30 seconds".

Assets to supply

  • Logo — SVG of the full lockup and the "A" mark alone, on transparent.
  • App icon set — 1024px master of the "A" mark on --bg; web favicon; TV tile art (Roku/Fire/Android sizes).
  • Channel logos — one per channel, SVG or PNG on transparent, safe on dark.
  • Slate / ident — a still "Asiko TV" holding frame for signal loss and pre-roll.
  • Sponsor placeholder — the house-promo creative shown when an avail is unsold.
  • Fonts — none needed; Poppins + Inter are free (OFL), embedded from Google Fonts on web and bundled in TV builds.
  • Colour — none needed; tokens above are final and match asiko.live.
