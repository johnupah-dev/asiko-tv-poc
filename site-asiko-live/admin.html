Connecting a real CRM

Right now every form on this site (Advertise, List Your Channel, Join As A Creator, Test Run, Contact) captures a lead and, with no backend configured, opens a pre-filled email to john.upah@mediaiconsltd.com so nothing is lost. To make this dashboard persistent and multi-user — and to feed leads straight into marky.ai or another CRM — open assets/js/leads.js and set CRM_ENDPOINT to your webhook or API URL. Every submission will then POST as JSON automatically, with the email fallback kept as a safety net if that request ever fails.
