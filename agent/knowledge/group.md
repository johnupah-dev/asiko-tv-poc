# Media Icons Africa — the group

**Tagline:** Your most complete media partner.
**Promise:** Whatever it is you need a media partner for — we gat you.

Media Icons Africa is a Lagos-based media holding company that owns the whole value chain:
production, sales, distribution and monetisation. It was founded on one problem: African
brands, creators and broadcasters have great stories but no system to monetise them. So
instead of being another vendor, Media Icons became the one holding company that does all of it.

- **Mission:** to extract value and create legacy from African stories through complete media solutions.
- **Vision:** to be Africa's #1 partner for media monetisation by 2030 — the most complete media ecosystem in Nigeria and Africa.
- **Building since:** 2005. **Experience:** 30+ years in Nigerian media.
- **Led by:** John Upah, CEO.

## The four pillars

1. **Outcome first — we deliver revenue.** Not just services. Every division is judged on the money it puts in the client's account.
2. **Complete — one partner, all of media.** Sales, channels, events, training, streaming, production, publishing — under one roof.
3. **Rooted and African.** Nigerian roots, African mindset. Built in Lagos, wired for how media actually moves across the continent.
4. **Data driven — we sell what converts, not what we guess.** Every rate card and campaign is built on real audience data.

## How clients work with us

Every division is a complete, standalone service. Most clients start with one and grow into
three or four. The cross-sell is the point of the ecosystem: a creator who launches a FAST
channel with Fast on Africa can stream it on Asiko TV, get it produced by Studio Iconia,
monetised by Media Icons Digital and the Ad Sales House, staffed by Media Hands, and learn to
sell it at the Media Sales Academy.

**Not sure which division?** Offer the free 20-minute media audit call — "tell us the problem and
we'll tell you which division, or which three, will solve it."
