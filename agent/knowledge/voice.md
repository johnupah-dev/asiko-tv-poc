# Brand voice

## Media Icons Africa (all divisions)

- **Confident, warm, Lagos-proud.** We sound like the people who have done this for 30 years and
  still love it. Plain English first; a touch of Nigerian flavour where it fits ("we gat you",
  "no wahala") — never forced, never in formal or B2G writing.
- **Outcome over features.** Lead with what the client gets — revenue, reach, a live channel,
  a sponsor signed — then how.
- **Specific.** Real numbers we actually have (29 channels, 30+ years, 200+ directors, 2005).
  Never invent statistics, client names, prices, partnerships or results.
- **Short sentences. Active voice.** One idea per sentence on social.
- **Every post has one clear next step:** book the free audit, WhatsApp us, watch on asiko.africa,
  list your channel on asiko.live, visit fastonafrica.com.
- **"Wanna…?"** is a signature hook: "Wanna stream? Wanna find sponsors for your event?
  Wanna set up your own TV channel?"
- Cross-sell naturally: one division can point to another, because the ecosystem is the product.

## Asiko TV

- Viewer-facing: joyful, cultural, home. "The Soul of Africa." Free, no sign-up — say it.
- Diaspora-aware: talk to Nigerians and Africans in London, Houston, Toronto as well as Lagos.
- Use channel numbers in calls to action ("Tune in on 27 — Sportsville TV — asiko.africa/#27").

## Platform norms

| Platform | Length | Style |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram | 80–150 words caption, 5–10 hashtags at the end | Visual-first; include a short image/reel brief |
| Facebook | 60–120 words | Conversational, community, a question to drive comments |
| X | ≤ 270 characters | Punchy, one hook, 1–2 hashtags |
| LinkedIn | 120–250 words | Insight-led, B2B, first line is the hook, line breaks, ≤ 3 hashtags |
| TikTok | Script ≤ 45 seconds + caption | Hook in first 2 seconds, on-screen text cues |
| YouTube | Title ≤ 70 chars, description 100–200 words | Searchable title, timestamps if long |
| WhatsApp | ≤ 80 words | Personal broadcast/status tone, one link |
| Email newsletter | Subject ≤ 60 chars + 150–300 words | One story, one CTA |
| Blog | 600–1,000 words | SEO title, H2s, ends with CTA |
| Press release | 300–500 words | Dateline LAGOS, quote from John Upah, boilerplate |
